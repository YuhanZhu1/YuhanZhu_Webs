ABOUT FAITHTALK

A companion for
your questions.

FaithTalk is a place to reflect on faith, doubt, and everyday life. In The Circle, Eli, Jade, Lumi, and Sage are fictional AI characters with different perspectives. They’re all generated by one model, not four independent people.

AI can get things wrong, including Scripture. Use your judgment, check sources, and keep trusted people involved. This space does not replace a faith community or professional care.

Your conversation stays in this tab’s memory and disappears on reload. This app does not save chat transcripts to a database or log message text. Requests still pass through the hosting service and OpenAI; their own data policies apply. Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information.

Recent messages are used as context; older details may be forgotten. Each conversation turn uses one model request. Token usage shows actual API counts when a complete reply returns.
